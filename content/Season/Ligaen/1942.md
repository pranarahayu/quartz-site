The 1942 Mesterrækken was the inaugural season of the top tier of the Kyststedian football league system. Contested by eight teams, the season ended with Akademisk Boldklub winning its first championship.

### Format

The three best teams from the Kyststed Hovedstad Championship and the champions of the other regional championships were invited to the inaugural season of Kyststed's first unified national league system. Each team played the others twice for a total of 14 matches.
