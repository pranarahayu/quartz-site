The **1945 Mesterrækken** was the third season of the top tier of the Kyststedian football league system, returning after a one-year hiatus due to World War II. Contested by eight teams, the season concluded with ØBK Østereng claiming their maiden national championship, finishing two points clear of Akademisk Boldklub.
